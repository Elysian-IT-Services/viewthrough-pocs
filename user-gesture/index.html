User gesture

This POC tests how long a click keeps the right to open a window. It worked: calling window.open straight away inside the click handler is allowed, and the same call made a second and a half later is blocked by the popup blocker, because the activation the click granted has already expired. Every pop-under POC in this repo depends on that first case, which is why they all open the window before doing anything else.

How to test

  1. Press Open immediately. Expect a small window on example.com and the log below to say the window opened.
  2. Close that window and press Open after a delay. Expect no window, and the log to say the popup blocker stopped it.
  3. Look at the browser's address bar for the blocked-popup icon. Chrome and Firefox both show one after the second test.
  4. If your browser allowed the delayed window too, check whether you have popups allowed for localhost, then try again in a fresh profile.
