Opener isolation

What a new tab can still do to the tab that opened it. rel="noopener" cuts the handle — the same handle the pop-unders rely on.

Test it

  1. Press Open without noopener: the new tab reports a live window.opener and navigates this tab away.
  2. Come back, press Open with noopener: the new tab reports null and this tab is untouched.
