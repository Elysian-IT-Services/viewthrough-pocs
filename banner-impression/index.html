Banner impression

This POC loads 's real creative straight from their CDN, so the impression is requested by the reader's browser instead of by our server. It half worked: impressions were counted, but they landed under Default rather than our source, the cookie on the response was rejected, and nothing ties the person who saw the banner to a registration later on.

How to test

  1. Open DevTools, go to the Network tab, tick Disable cache, then reload this page. A cached image makes no request, so nothing gets counted.
  2. Find the request to in the Network list. Expect status 200.
  3. Open that request's Cookies tab. Expect a cookie named after the creative id to be listed as blocked — the response carries no SameSite=None; Secure, so the browser drops it on a cross-site image.
  4. Open , find the Media Report and read the Impressions column, not Visitors. Expect the count to rise under Default, not under our source.
  5. Register a test account afterwards and look for it in the Registrations Report. Expect nothing: no impression was ever linked to a person.
  6. Do not click the banner. A click fires the visit URL and adds a second attribution path, which ruins the result.
